  1. Home

Project Overview

Remodeling of Secondary SWD V-112 from Milk Colony to 17th Cross of Malleshwaram, including construction of RC Box Drain from Yeshwanthpura Bridge to Railway culvert and inside Milk Colony (Vrishabhavathi Valley Div-1).

Key Deliverables

RC box drains, culvert interfacing, hydraulic improvements to mitigate flooding and enhance urban stormwater conveyance.
