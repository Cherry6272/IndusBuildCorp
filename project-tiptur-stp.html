Waste Stabilisation Pond – Tiptur STP

Underground Drainage System in Tiptur CMC package no. TPT-1 (STP & Wet Well), KUIDFC.

Enlarge Spec Photo
Enlarge Spec Photo

Project Overview

Waste Stabilisation Pond work executed for the comprehensive underground drainage system in Tiptur City Municipal Council under Package No. TPT-1 (STP & wet well).

This critical civic sewage treatment project encompassed the construction of anaerobic and facultative stabilisation ponds, reinforced circular wet wells, influent pumping stations, and associated effluent management infrastructure, establishing reliable, environmentally compliant wastewater sanitation for Tiptur town.

Key Deliverables & Scope

  • Complete sewage treatment plant with multi-cell Waste Stabilisation Ponds (WSP)
  • Heavy RCC circular wet well construction and intermediate sewage pumping facilities
  • Seamless integration with municipal underground drainage (UGD) collector networks
  • Environmental compliance systems meeting Karnataka State Pollution Control Board guidelines
  • On-site effluent testing, quality control, and discharge monitoring structures

Technical Specifications

Treatment Technology Biological Waste Stabilisation Ponds (Anaerobic & Facultative Cells)
Intake Structure RCC Circular Wet Well & Screen Chamber Pumping Station
Network Integration Integrated with Tiptur CMC Package TPT-1 UGD Trunk Mains
Environmental Mandate KUIDFC Environmental & Social Management Framework (ESMF)

Project Specifications

  • Contract Value 3.0 Crores (Scheme Total: 7.5 Cr)
  • Location Tiptur Town, Tumakuru District, Karnataka
  • Project Year 2011-12
  • Category Construction (STP & Wet Well)
  • Authority / Client KUIDFC / CMC Tiptur
  • Main Contractor M/s. KBR Infratech Limited
  • Status Completed
