Project Overview

Comprehensive Infrastructure Improvement Project (CIIP) proposals covering rigid Cement Concrete (CC) roads, storm water drains, and associated civic works executed across multiple municipal divisions under the Mangalore Municipal Corporation (MMC).

Operating in coastal Karnataka's heavy monsoon environment required high-durability mix designs, anti-erosion drain profiling, and engineered concrete curing methodologies to ensure lasting structural resilience.

Key Deliverables & Scope

  • Construction of heavy-duty Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) drains
  • High-grade Cement Concrete (CC) road carriageway paving with dowel and tie-bar joints
  • Municipal division-wide road widening, grading, and surface drainage channels
  • Storm water discharge outlets preventing coastal inundation during heavy monsoons
